Monocurl

A language for mathematical animation. Declare meshes, assign states, play transitions — the runtime interpolates geometry between them.

Start learning Open stdlib

Declarative

Assign a new value to a mesh leader and call a transition primitive. The runtime determines how to move geometry between mathematical states — no keyframes, no timeline editors.

Mathematical

Meshes are first-class values: curves, surfaces, labels, or trees of other meshes. Syntax is close to Rust in structure and close to math notation in how vectors and transformations read.

Live

Scenes render as a continuous video or step through as a slideshow. The editor previews the scene continuously up to the current timestamp as you type.
